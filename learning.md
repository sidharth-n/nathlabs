# Learning

## 2026-09-17: Discover demand before measuring keywords

**Context:** The first research plan put DataForSEO before customer-language discovery, and Sid corrected the sequence.
**Lesson:** Mine real conversations and paid-work briefs first, extract repeated problems and exact language, then use those findings as seeds for DataForSEO. Keyword tools validate demand; they do not invent the market thesis.
**Example:** Find UAE operators asking for lead qualification, document processing or support automation on Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn and freelance briefs before checking search volume for those phrases.
**Applies when:** Researching service pages, SaaS opportunities, SEO clusters or any unfamiliar market.
