# Learning

## 2026-09-17: Discover demand before measuring keywords

**Context:** The first research plan put DataForSEO before customer-language discovery, and Sid corrected the sequence.
**Lesson:** Mine real conversations and paid-work briefs first, extract repeated problems and exact language, then use those findings as seeds for DataForSEO. Keyword tools validate demand; they do not invent the market thesis.
**Example:** Find UAE operators asking for lead qualification, document processing or support automation on Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn and freelance briefs before checking search volume for those phrases.
**Applies when:** Researching service pages, SaaS opportunities, SEO clusters or any unfamiliar market.

## 2026-09-17: Research guides offers; it is not company copy

**Context:** The first website exposed keyword volumes, CPC, hiring signals and internal research counts in sales pages. Sid rejected the result and requested a complete redesign.
**Lesson:** Use demand evidence to choose services and customer language. Public pages explain the buyer's problem, the proposed workflow, delivery scope, limitations and a clear next step. Keep market analysis in the research directory. No invented results or client proof, and no double hyphens or em dashes in prose.
**Example:** A WhatsApp service page explains lead capture, approved answers and staff handoff instead of quoting search volume for WhatsApp automation.
**Applies when:** Creating any company website or sales content from research.

## 2026-09-17: Keep the payments goal ahead of cosmetic work

**Context:** Sid parked four new logo concepts to launch the company website and move to Stripe activation.
**Lesson:** Keep the current approved branding when cosmetic exploration delays the active business goal. Save alternatives and resume only when requested.
**Example:** Publish the verified Natha Labs website with its existing N logo, then start a dedicated Stripe session.
**Applies when:** Prioritizing website polish against launch or account activation.

## 2026-09-17: Match canonical URLs to the production primary domain

**Context:** The existing apex domain redirected to www, while rebuilt metadata initially used the apex.
**Lesson:** Inspect live domain configuration before releasing canonical, schema and sitemap changes. Keep every absolute search URL consistent with the actual primary domain.
**Example:** Natha Labs uses https://www.nathalabs.com throughout, preserving the existing redirect.
**Applies when:** Releasing a rebuilt site onto an existing production domain.
